# Medhira Research

### Independent Student-Led Research & Learning Collective

> Exploring intelligence. Engineering evidence.

Medhira Research is an independent, student-led space for learning, experimentation, collaboration, and research exploration.

We believe that everyone starts somewhere. Whether you're learning how to use GitHub, writing your first program, exploring Linux, entering research, or building your first project, you're welcome here.

## What We Explore

- Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
- Computer Vision & Image Forensics
- Cloud & Backend Engineering
- Linux & Open Source
- Research & Reproducible Experimentation
- Beginner-Friendly Technology Education

## Learn. Build. Connect.

Medhira Research is designed for curious students who want to:

- Understand technology from the ground up
- Explore different career and learning paths
- Build practical projects
- Learn through experimentation
- Discover open-source opportunities
- Connect with peers, seniors, and potential collaborators
- Share knowledge and learn together

## Our Community

Curiosity is encouraged. Experimentation is welcome. Friendly chaos is part of the culture.

You can communicate in English, Telugu, Hindi, Bengali, or any language that helps you connect comfortably.

We support respectful discussions, constructive feedback, collaborative learning, and a healthy balance between work and fun.
